# LLMWiki Workflow

- Before starting any task, read `llmwiki/index.md` and the relevant LLMWiki pages to understand the project's current context, decisions, and prior work.
- After completing a task, record the changes, decisions, and verification results in LLMWiki, then update `llmwiki/index.md` and `llmwiki/log.md` when applicable.
